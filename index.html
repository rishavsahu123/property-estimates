Your data stays on this phone. If you uninstall the app or clear Chrome’s data, it’s gone. Save a full backup from the download menu now and then.

PDF or Excel? The PDF shows every slip photo and opens anywhere. Excel photos show in the Excel or WPS app, not in WhatsApp or Google Drive previews.

Restore adds entries from a backup file and keeps everything already on this phone. A backup from the old Ghar ka Hisaab app works too.

All, Construction, Documents next to the project name filters the whole app. Each group’s category is set in Edit groups on the Add screen. Groups marked Unknown show only under All.

Receipts: in Passbook tap Select, pick entries, then Share receipt.
